## Round 2: test 4, the decimal counter, with its wrong twin

- **What the owner asked:** build test 4, the decimal counter with its wrong twin. Make a passing version and a version that fails, as in tests 2 and 3. Show which part of the review and the documents leads to this test.
- **The A side:** `src/bcd.inc` holds two routines that the game and the test share. `bcd_inc` adds 1 with `add al, 1` and `daa` on the low byte, then `adc al, 0` and `daa` on the high byte. After 9999 comes 0000, with CF = 1. `bcd_digits` changes each nibble into a character with `XLAT`. `src/counter.asm` shows four large digits from the 8 x 8 font of the BIOS, the 16 bits under them, and a carry cell. The key J jumps to 9990. `COUNTER.COM` is 623 bytes.
- **The shared keys:** `src/common.inc` reads the key J only when the program sets `JUMP_KEY`. `DOT3D.COM` and `SPIN3D.COM` keep their sizes, and their checks still pass.
- **The test:** `test/checkbcd.asm` runs `bcd_inc` 10,000 times and writes `BCD.BIN`: 10,000 values, the value after 9999, and the carry (20,003 bytes).
- **The B side:** `lab/check_bcd.f90` makes each value from the number of steps with `MOD` and `ISHFT`, and compares all of them. It also requires 0000 and a carry of 1 after 9999.
- **The result:** the two sides agree on 10,000 of 10,000 values, and the step after 9999 is right.
- **The wrong twin:** the switch `-dWRONG_DECIMAL` removes the two `DAA` words. The counter then counts in binary: 0009, 000A, 000B. The check finds 9,990 wrong values, the number that PLAN.md gave before the code existed. After 9999 steps the counter is 2710h with no carry. `build.sh` requires this check to fail.
- **The pages:** `/game/test4a/` and `/game/test4b/`, with the A and B panels. The panels read the 16 bit cells and the carry cell from the screen of the emulator, so they follow the counter. In Chrome, the panels followed the counter, the key J went to 9990, and the counter went from 9999 to 0000. The page `/game/` lists the two new tests.
- **The path from the review:** the page of test 4a has the section "Where this test comes from". It links the moment in the video (32:05), the findings D13-M11, D15-M7, D14-M1 and D15-M1, learning unit U3 in the summary, the segment pages that teach `DAA`, the design in PLAN.md (Phase 4d), and the dictionary words. The page of test 4b links the rule of the wrong twin.
- **A note for later:** on the game pages, the first click on a button sometimes does nothing, and the browser reports that `simulateKeyPress` is not a function. The pages of test 3 do the same. The next clicks work. This change does not fix it.
