\#\#\#\#\# Replicator Mass Tiers & Data Storage Limits A functional replicator requires sufficient internal memory to store its operating intelligence, operating system, and industrial design blueprints. \* **DNA Storage Density:** Biological systems demonstrate an average data density of \$9.13 \\times 10^{20} \\text{ bits/g}\$ (equivalent to 1 bit per \$1.10 \\times 10^{-24} \\text{ kg}\$ ). \* **Theoretical Carbon Diamond Storage:** Encoding binary data into a diamond lattice using carbon-12 and carbon-13 isotopes yields an upper-limit storage density of \$5 \\times 10^{22} \\text{ bits/g}\$ . At this limit, a single gram of structured diamond could hold \$50,000 \\text{ Exabytes}\$ —enough to house humanity's total digital data footprint alongside millions of digital mind emulations.
