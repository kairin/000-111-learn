\#\#\#\# 3\. Infrastructure & Propulsion: Dyson Swarms and Rocketry Accelerating millions of intergalactic probes to relativistic velocities requires energy scales far beyond planetary reserves. Such energy is harvested directly from a host star by constructing a **Dyson Swarm** —a vast system of independent solar-collecting satellites and thermal engines orbiting in a dense formation around the star.
