\#\#\#\#\# Evaluating Traditional Paradox Resolutions This intergalactic expansion model sharpens the Fermi Paradox by over eight orders of magnitude, forcing a rigorous re-evaluation of traditional resolutions: \* **Behavioral Convergence Hypotheses are Severely Weakened:** Attributing the Great Silence to non-expansionist choices (e.g., "aliens choose to live quietly") requires *absolute, unbroken motivational uniformity* across millions of independent origin galaxies lacking causal contact. This degree of universal social convergence is statistically implausible. \* **First-Mover Hegemony & The "Already Here" Resolution are Integrated:** Because universal probe deployment requires negligible energy, an early "first-mover" civilization emerging billions of years ago could have easily saturated all reachable galaxies with quiet, unobtrusive "police probes" or interdiction hardware. This scenario—the *Probe Hegemony / Interdict Hypothesis* —resolves the paradox without requiring ongoing social convergence across latecomers: the first mover enforces a "quiet" local volume by interdicting new expansion projects or maintaining strict non-interference protocols. \* **The Early Great Filter is Dramatically Strengthened:** Because intergalactic expansion is physically trivial and energy-cheap for any Dyson-capable species, the absence of extraterrestrial probes in our solar system strongly implies that intelligent life fails to emerge in the first place. Either abiogenesis and complex intelligence are unimaginably rare ( **Early Great Filter** ), or technological species almost universally destroy themselves prior to deploying their first probe fleets ( **Late Great Filter** ). \* **First-Mover Status & Existential Responsibility:** If humanity is not facing an imminent Late Filter and is instead among the very first technological species to emerge in our cosmological horizon, we possess an extraordinary, unprecedented **First-Mover Status** .
